# Contact / Help

## Email support

`support@revealforge.com`

This is a small, focused product line — email is how to reach us.

## Before you write in, please have ready

To help us solve your issue on the first reply, include:

- **Your Blender version** (e.g. "4.5.13 LTS" or "5.2.1 LTS" — see
  `Help > About Blender`).
- **Whether the add-on is installed** (Edit > Preferences > Get
  Extensions) or you're using the node group on its own.
- **Your operating system** (Windows / macOS / Linux, and version).
- **A screenshot** of the issue (viewport, panel, modifier stack, or
  error message, whichever is relevant).
- **A minimal `.blend` file** that reproduces the problem, if you can
  put one together — even a stripped-down version of your scene with
  just the anchor points object and the modifier applied helps us
  diagnose faster than a description alone.

## What we can help with

- Installation and setup issues, whether you're using the add-on or the
  node group on its own — see the [User Guide](guide.md).
- Questions about how the panel, node group, or workflow are meant to
  be used — see the [User Guide](guide.md) and [FAQ](faq.md) first,
  most common questions are already answered there.
- Compatibility questions for your specific Blender version — see
  [Compatibility](compatibility.md).
- License and usage-rights questions — see [License](license.md).

## What's out of scope

- Custom scene setup or scripting work on your specific project (we can
  point you to the relevant guide section, but can't build your scene
  for you).
- Support for modified/redistributed copies of the asset outside the
  terms of the [License](license.md).
- Issues caused by third-party add-ons or renderer-specific bugs
  unrelated to ExploCallout itself.

## Before buying

Purchase details, including where to buy and the refund policy, will be
published here at launch. If you have a question before purchasing in
the meantime, the [FAQ](faq.md) covers common product questions
(compatibility, licensing for client work). For anything else, email us
at `support@revealforge.com` — pre-purchase questions are welcome.
