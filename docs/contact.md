# Contact / Help

## Email support

`support@revealforge.com`

We aim to respond within **48 business hours** (Monday–Friday, excluding
public holidays). This is a small, focused product line — email is the
fastest and most reliable way to reach us, independent of any
marketplace messaging system.

## Before you write in, please have ready

To help us solve your issue on the first reply, include:

- **Your Blender version** (e.g. "4.5.13 LTS" or "5.2.1 LTS" — see
  `Help > About Blender`).
- **Your operating system** (Windows / macOS / Linux, and version).
- **A screenshot** of the issue (viewport, modifier panel, or error
  message, whichever is relevant).
- **A minimal `.blend` file** that reproduces the problem, if you can
  put one together — even a stripped-down version of your scene with
  just the anchor points object and the modifier applied helps us
  diagnose faster than a description alone.

## What we can help with

- Installation and setup issues (Asset Library, Append/Link).
- Questions about how the node group and workflow are meant to be used
  — see the [User Guide](guide.md) and [FAQ](faq.md) first, most
  common questions are already answered there.
- Compatibility questions for your specific Blender version — see
  [Compatibility](compatibility.md).
- License and usage-rights questions — see [License](license.md).
- Purchase and refund questions — refunds follow Superhive's standard
  marketplace policy; check your order/receipt page first, then reach
  out if anything's unclear.

## What's out of scope

- Custom scene setup or scripting work on your specific project (we can
  point you to the relevant guide section, but can't build your scene
  for you).
- Support for modified/redistributed copies of the asset outside the
  terms of the [License](license.md).
- Issues caused by third-party add-ons or renderer-specific bugs
  unrelated to the node group itself.

## Before buying

If you have a question before purchasing, the [FAQ](faq.md) covers the
most common ones (compatibility, licensing for client work, refund
policy). If your question isn't answered there, email us at
`support@revealforge.com` — pre-purchase questions are welcome.
